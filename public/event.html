The Basics

What to know before you come

Weather Protocol

Late October, El Niño year — we're watching it. One week out, we make the call. Mild rain or sprinkles → party's on. Anything worse → we'll tell you to sit this one out, and refund your ticket. Refund window closes the day before. Last year ran cold and cloudy, 50s–60s — bring layers regardless.

Dress Code

Three fits: kickball/farm daytime, colorful dance nighttime, fall farmers market. Bring a swimsuit and towel — you never know.

Bring

Camping gear (tent, sleeping bag, pad, pillow, light, chair, cooler), a musical instrument if you've got one, water bottle + beer cup, lunch for Saturday, arts and crafts supplies, and old vegetables for the tortoises. Dogs welcome.

🌮 Dinner's from Jorge's taco crew — chicken, carnitas, veggie. 100% goes to Jorge and Moises. Cash tips appreciated, not required.
☀️🍻 cheers to our talented music friends, chef Jorge, sous chef Moises, Mr. Manual the groundskeeper, and the Gragg family for letting us vibe hard on this lil citrus orchard oasis.
