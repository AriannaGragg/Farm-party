How it works

Three tickets, one wristband, zero surprises.

01

Claim a spot

Pay by Venmo, enter your handle at checkout. We confirm manually — your account flips to Verified once we see it land.

02

Pick your role

Musician, volunteer, or guest — each comes with its own perks, and volunteers get first pick of jobs: keg, golf cart, cleanup.

03

Get stamped

Show up, get your wristband at the gate. No stamp, no entry — that's how we keep it to the people who are actually coming.
